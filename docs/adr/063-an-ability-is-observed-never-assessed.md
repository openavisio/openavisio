# An Ability is observed, never assessed

In Dup15q, formal cognitive tests (Wechsler, SON-R) often end without a result: the child refuses, loses attention or avoids the exam, and shows at home what it would not show in the test room. The association's registry collects who was testable, "not testable" included, and research cohorts fall back on a parent interview (Vineland) for adaptive behaviour. A family that arrives with dated, concrete examples answers such an interview better. Milestones existed as a category of Event with a domain, but a milestone is one "first time": it cannot show that a child put on her socks in March and no longer in June. Issues #464 and #465. Terms are in `GLOSSARY.md` under "Development".

## The decision

**Abilities get their own identity.** An Ability is named by the family and holds dated Ability Observations: where, works or not, on its own or with help, and from what source. The Observation history shows how an Ability changes, losses included. "No longer" is derived from that history, never stored.

**Milestones are absorbed.** A first word is the first Observation of an Ability that works. The milestone category and its domain field on Events go; existing milestones become Abilities with one Observation, the milestone's description its note, so nothing a family wrote is lost.

**Development questions on an Intake Form are answered from Abilities.** "When did your child start walking?" was a History Fact in practice, though the glossary sent it to the milestone. Now the answer is an Ability Observation with source "Intake Form", and the next form reads it back. Everything else that is history stays a History Fact (ADR 047).

**A Test Attempt records how a test went, not its score.** Test, date, who ran it, setting, duration, and how it ended with the reason. A completed test's score is in its report, linked as a Document.

**Nothing is scored or compared.** No age norms, no developmental level, no items or scales from Vineland or any other licensed instrument; the five domains are plain groupings. The AI Health Assistant quotes single Observations with date, place and source and never sums them up.

**Only therapy reports are mined, and only for their current state** (#465). Anamnesis sections retell the same history in every letter, so they stay unread for the same reason as in ADR 047. An Observation proposed from a report keeps the report as its source, so "works at home, not at the SPZ" stays visible.

## Alternatives, and why not

- **Build the Vineland in.** Its items and norms are licensed, it is given by trained examiners, and a rebuilt version with own items would no longer count for research.
- **A status list per Ability** (can / sometimes / no longer). Compact, but a state without evidence, and it reads like a questionnaire.
- **Extend milestones with a status.** Keeps a developmental record inside the calendar, where it never belonged.
- **Enter the test score.** A number without its report is the figure psychologists warn against reading alone.

## Consequences

- The doctor and authority Handouts gain Ability Observations and Test Attempts of a chosen period: a Medizinischer Dienst assessment or a Widerspruch turns on what a child does on its own or with help. Emergency and day-to-day Handouts do not.
- A sidebar entry "Entwicklung" changes the smoke test (ADR 033).
