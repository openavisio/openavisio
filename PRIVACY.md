# OpenAvisio Mobile Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-09_

The OpenAvisio mobile app is a thin client for a **self-hosted OpenAvisio server**
that you or your organization run and control. This policy covers the **app**. The
health data you view through it lives on your server and is governed by whoever
operates that server, not by the app and not by its developer.

## What the app collects

**Nothing is sent to the developer.** The app contains no analytics, no telemetry,
no advertising, and no tracking.

The app stores **on your device only**:

- the **Deployment URL** (the address of your OpenAvisio server), so it can reconnect
  on the next launch, and
- your **app-lock preference** (whether biometric/device unlock is required to open
  the app).

Both are removed when you uninstall the app or use the in-app reset.

## Network connections

The app connects **only** to the OpenAvisio server whose URL you enter. All health
data is exchanged directly between your device and that server. The app makes no
other network requests.

## Biometrics

If you enable the optional app lock, biometric/device-credential verification is
handled entirely by your device's operating system. The app never sees or stores
your fingerprint, face, or PIN.

## Data on your server

Any collection, use, or sharing of health data happens on the OpenAvisio server you
connect to. The operator of that server (the "Organization") is the controller of
that data. Contact that operator for questions about it.
